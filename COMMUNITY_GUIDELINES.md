# Community Guidelines

We want this project to be a welcoming place for players, add-on creators, testers, and contributors of all experience levels. These guidelines apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Help the community work well

- Be kind, patient, and specific.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking the person behind them.
- Assume honest mistakes while remaining open to correction.
- Give constructive feedback with enough detail to act on it.
- Respect different skill levels, languages, identities, and accessibility needs.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses and creator requests.
- Keep posts relevant and use clear titles so information is easy to find.
- Protect privacy: remove tokens, private logs, personal information, and private conversations before posting.

## Add-ons and support

- Describe an add-on's purpose, dependencies, permissions, risks, and limitations honestly.
- Clearly label experimental, mature, flashing, frightening, or otherwise sensitive content where relevant.
- Do not pressure maintainers or creators for release dates or immediate support.
- Report bugs with reproducible details, and allow creators a reasonable chance to respond.
- Do not shame users for asking basic questions or choosing not to install an add-on.
- Add-on publication is governed by [Publishing Add-ons](PUBLISHING_ADDONS.md).

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualized attention, doxxing, spam, impersonation, deliberate disruption, and encouraging harm are not allowed. Neither are malicious submissions, knowingly false reports, coordinated abuse, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

Disagreement is welcome; hostility is not. Repeatedly reopening settled arguments, derailing unrelated threads, or contacting people after they ask you to stop may be treated as disruption.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban someone when needed to protect the project and its community. The response will be based on context, severity, impact, and patterns of behavior.

For reporting and enforcement details, see the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#reporting-a-problem).
