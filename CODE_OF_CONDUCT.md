# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in NuttyMod Loader for The Cube Beta a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a positive community include:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community; and
- following the project's [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md) and [add-on publishing rules](PUBLISHING_ADDONS.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment, threats, or intimidation;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- malicious code, deceptive add-on behavior, or attempts to compromise users or project infrastructure;
- retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith; and
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when a person is officially representing the project in public spaces. Project spaces include repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other community channels managed by the project.

## Reporting a problem

Report abusive behavior, malicious packages, or other sensitive concerns privately to the repository maintainers using an available private contact method on their GitHub profiles or organization page. Include links, screenshots, dates, and a concise description when possible.

If no private contact method is available, open a brief issue asking a maintainer for private follow-up. Do not include sensitive details, exploit instructions, personal information, or evidence that could put someone at risk in the public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as promptly and discreetly as reasonably possible. Information may be shared only with people needed to investigate and respond. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest should hand the report to another maintainer when possible. A knowingly false or retaliatory report may itself violate this Code of Conduct; a report made in good faith will not be penalized merely because it cannot be confirmed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Depending on the context, severity, impact, and history, they may:

1. provide a private correction or warning;
2. remove or edit content and require corrective action;
3. temporarily restrict participation;
4. reject or remove an add-on or contribution; or
5. permanently ban participation in project spaces.

Immediate action may be taken when necessary to protect people, accounts, systems, or data. Maintainers will aim for fair and consistent enforcement but may skip steps when an incident is severe.
